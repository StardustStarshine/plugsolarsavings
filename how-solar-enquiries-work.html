How Solar Enquiries Work

What happens when you ask Plug Solar Savings for help exploring solar options.

Why submit an enquiry?

You may submit your details if you want to explore solar options, ask for further assistance, understand possible installation choices or be contacted about a suitable solar solution.

What information is used?

We may use the information you provide, such as your name, contact details, postcode, property information, energy-use answers, preferred solar option and timeframe, together with relevant calculator results. This helps us understand the nature of your request.

What happens next?

  1. We receive your request through the website.
  2. We may review the information to understand whether it is suitable for further assistance.
  3. Where appropriate, we may share the relevant details with a relevant solar provider.
  4. The provider may contact you about your enquiry and possible options.

Will every enquiry be sent to installers?

No. We aim to make relevant introductions rather than send every enquiry to installers. Whether an introduction is made depends on the information supplied, the type of assistance requested and whether a suitable provider is available.

Do I have to buy anything?

No. Submitting an enquiry does not obligate you to buy a product or service. You should make your own decision, compare options and check any quotation or provider information carefully.

How does Plug Solar Savings earn money?

Plug Solar Savings may receive payment for a qualified enquiry or referral. This commercial relationship is one reason we explain the process openly. It does not mean that a provider is guaranteed to be suitable or that you must accept an offer.

Your choices and privacy

Details are handled as described in our Privacy Policy. The calculator/contact form should clearly explain any potential sharing before submission. If separate marketing consent is requested, it should be optional, specific and separate from consent needed to respond to the enquiry.
