Contact FAQs

A few things worth knowing before getting in touch.

Can you provide a quote for solar?

Plug Solar Savings provides information, estimates and comparisons rather than installer quotes. Our calculator is designed to give you an indication of potential savings, not a property-specific survey or quotation.

Can you tell me which solar system is right for my home?

We can help you understand the factors that affect different solar options, but information on this website should not be treated as a technical assessment of your property.

I found an error on the website. What should I do?

Please contact us with the page, product or information you believe needs correcting. If you have a reliable source for the correction, including it will help us check the information.

Can I suggest a topic for the website?

Yes. Suggestions for practical questions about plug-in solar, electricity savings, appliances, batteries, products and installation are welcome.

Is the calculator an installer assessment?

No. The calculator provides an estimate for guidance only. Actual generation and savings depend on factors including location, panel position, shading, equipment, electricity tariff and household energy use.
