About Plug Solar Savings

Helping UK homeowners understand their solar options.

What Plug Solar Savings does

Plug Solar Savings is an independent UK website helping homeowners understand solar energy, compare different solar options and estimate what solar could potentially save them.

We aim to make solar information easier to understand, without assuming that every home needs the same system or that every advertised saving will apply to every household.

What we cover

  • Plug-in solar
  • Rooftop solar
  • Solar batteries and storage
  • Solar panels, inverters and other components
  • Solar savings and electricity use
  • Practical UK solar information, rules and considerations

Our calculator

Our calculator uses information supplied by a visitor to produce an illustrative estimate. It is not a property survey, professional energy assessment, technical design, installation quotation or guarantee of savings. Actual results depend on the equipment, installation, location, sunlight, tariff, household behaviour and other factors.

How the site may earn money

Some pages may contain product links that could generate affiliate commission. Where appropriate, we may also introduce homeowners who request further help to a relevant solar provider. We may receive payment for qualified enquiries or referrals. These commercial relationships do not change the purpose of our educational content or mean that a particular product or provider is right for every visitor.

Our approach

We aim to be clear about estimates, commercial relationships and what happens when someone submits an enquiry. Visitors should always check product details, suitability, permissions, installation requirements and independent professional advice before making a purchase or installation decision.
