Privacy Policy

How Plug Solar Savings may collect, use and protect personal information.

Who we are

Plug Solar Savings operates this website to provide solar information, savings estimates and, where appropriate, introductions to solar providers.

Privacy contact:

For questions about cookies, Contact.

Information we may collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect information such as your name, email address, telephone number, postcode, homeowner status, property type, electricity usage, electricity bill information, solar interest, intended timeframe, roof or installation orientation, shading, daytime electricity use, calculator answers and results, and other information included in a contact or enquiry.

We may also receive technical information such as IP address, browser/device information and website usage data where this is collected by the technologies actually enabled on the site.

Why we use information

  • To operate the calculator and provide an illustrative estimate.
  • To respond to contact messages and requests.
  • To understand the solar option a visitor is interested in.
  • To assess whether an enquiry may be suitable for further assistance.
  • Where appropriate and transparently explained, to share enquiry details with a relevant solar provider so they can contact the person about the request.
  • To administer, secure and improve the website.
  • To manage affiliate, commercial or referral relationships where relevant.

Sharing information

We do not sell personal information indiscriminately. However, if you submit an enquiry seeking solar assistance, your details may be shared with a relevant solar provider where this is appropriate and permitted, in line with the wording shown at the point of submission and this policy. The provider may contact you about your enquiry.

We may also use service providers that process information for us, such as website hosting, form, automation, analytics or email services, but only where those services are actually used.

Lawful basis

The lawful basis depends on the specific processing. This may include taking steps at your request, performing or administering a requested service, legitimate interests, and consent where consent is required. For non-essential marketing communications or non-essential cookies, consent may be required and should be collected separately where applicable.

Retention

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose collected, including handling enquiries, maintaining records, meeting legal obligations, resolving disputes and preventing misuse.

Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect information. No online transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

Your rights

Subject to applicable law, you may have rights to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, portability or withdrawal of consent, and to complain to the UK Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). Requests can be sent to Contact.

Changes

We may update this policy when the website, suppliers or processing practices change. The latest version will be published on this page.
