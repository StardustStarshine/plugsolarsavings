How we calculated your estimate

We estimate the annual output of an 800W system using a UK regional solar-yield assumption, then adjust it for panel direction and shading.

We estimate how much of that electricity you could use directly based on when you normally use electricity.

We then apply an estimated electricity unit rate to the electricity you could use.

These are estimates rather than guarantees. Actual results depend on your exact location, installation, equipment, sunlight, electricity tariff and household behaviour.
