How we calculated your estimate

We estimate the annual output of an 800W plug-in solar system using a broad regional UK solar-yield assumption. We then adjust the estimate for panel direction and shading.

We estimate how much of the generated electricity could be used directly by your home based on when you normally use electricity. If you selected solar plus battery, we apply an additional illustrative allowance for increased solar self-consumption.

We then multiply the estimated solar electricity you can use by a benchmark electricity unit rate to estimate its potential value.

This is an estimate, not a guaranteed saving. Your actual result could be higher or lower depending on your exact location, panel position, shading, equipment, electricity tariff, household consumption and how much electricity you use while the panels are producing.
